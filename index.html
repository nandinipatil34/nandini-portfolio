⭐ Flagship Project
HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript Emergency System Responsive UI

Disaster Management Portal

A comprehensive disaster management web application created to assist communities and emergency responders during natural calamities (such as floods, earthquakes, and cyclones). Provides instant access to emergency helplines, live status updates, incident reporting, and shelter resource coordination.

  • Emergency Helplines Directory: Quick one-tap access to national & regional disaster response teams, ambulance, and police contacts.
  • Incident Reporting Form: Interactive form for victims to report affected locations, severity, and urgent needs with client-side validation.
  • Shelter & Relief Filter: Dynamic search and filter to find open relief camps, food distribution points, and medical centers.
  • Emergency Preparedness Guide: Offline-ready dos and don'ts guides for flood, earthquake, and fire safety.
